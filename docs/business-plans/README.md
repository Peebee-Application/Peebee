# Peebee module business plans

These plans are intended for investor and funding discussions. Founder-confirmed planning context as of 10 November 2026: Uganda is the market, Kampala and Entebbe come first, and no module has paying customers yet.

## First plan

[Shopping Concierge business plan](shopping-concierge/BUSINESS_PLAN.md) covers the one-rider shopping flow, customer pricing, platform income and three-year financial scenarios for Kampala and Entebbe.

The [shopping and delivery source audit](shopping-concierge/DELIVERY_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) traces the customer, rider, Admin, pricing and reporting paths. The [model script](shopping-concierge/model.py) reproduces the [financial scenarios](shopping-concierge/financial-model.json).

Each future module plan should separately explain the customer, existing product, market, competition, revenue, service operations, unit economics, shared costs, funding requirement and validation milestones. Group customer pools and shared costs must not be counted repeatedly in a consolidated Peebee proposal.

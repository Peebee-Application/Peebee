# Peebee module business plans

These plans are intended for investor and funding discussions. Founder-confirmed planning context as of 10 November 2026: Uganda is the market, Kampala and Entebbe come first, and no module has paying customers yet.

## First plan

[Shopping Concierge business plan](shopping-concierge/BUSINESS_PLAN.md) is a working investor draft revised after founder feedback and a deeper product review. It follows the existing platform's one-rider shopping and delivery flow. The first draft's proposed pricing, forecasts and funding amount are withdrawn. No replacement funding amount is recommended until actual commercial settings and costs are established.

The [shopping and delivery source audit](shopping-concierge/DELIVERY_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) traces the customer, rider, Admin, pricing and reporting paths. The original model files are retained with a withdrawn status for historical arithmetic only. Product source evidence is pinned in the plan. This documentation does not change any live application setting or behaviour.

Each future module plan should separately explain the customer, existing product, market, competition, revenue, service operations, unit economics, shared costs, funding requirement and validation milestones. Group customer pools and shared costs must not be counted repeatedly in a consolidated Peebee proposal.

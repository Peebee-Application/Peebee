# Peebee module business plans

These plans are intended for investor and funding discussions. Founder-confirmed planning context as of 10 November 2026: Uganda is the market, Kampala and Entebbe come first, and no module has paying customers yet.

## First plan

[Shopping Concierge business plan](shopping-concierge/BUSINESS_PLAN.md) is the first investor draft. It follows the existing platform's one-rider shopping and delivery flow. Its commercial inputs, funding amount, forecasts and pilot thresholds are proposed assumptions for review, not current settings or established traction.

The supporting model is reproducible from [model.py](shopping-concierge/model.py); [financial-model.json](shopping-concierge/financial-model.json) retains exact calculations and an 18 month cashflow. Product source evidence is pinned in the plan. This documentation does not change any live application setting or behaviour.

Each future module plan should separately explain the customer, existing product, market, competition, revenue, service operations, unit economics, shared costs, funding requirement and validation milestones. Group customer pools and shared costs must not be counted repeatedly in a consolidated Peebee proposal.
